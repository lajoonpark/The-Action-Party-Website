The Action Party / Financial disclosure

Financial disclosure

A party that asks for your vote and your money owes you the arithmetic behind both. This page is that arithmetic: what we take in, what we would spend, and who can check it.

01

Money in

What we take in

Membership is the whole of our income. One thousand dollars a year, per member, and nothing else. We do not take corporate donations, we do not take money from trusts that will not name their beneficiaries, and we do not sell access to anyone.

Where the party's income comes from, by share.
Source Share
Membership 92 percent
Donations under $1,000 8 percent
Corporate and trust donations 0 percent

Donations above one thousand dollars appear on the live register within seven days, with the amount and the date, as electoral law requires. Nothing is accepted anonymously.

02

Money out

What we would spend

Every clause in the register carries its own price and its own deadline. The four-year total, the change against current spending, and the revenue that pays for it are set out row by row on the finances page. The short version: we move money rather than invent it, and the totals balance.

  • Health: 600 billion by year three, against 32.7 billion now, paid for by the tax scale.
  • Housing and land: 1.2 trillion, against 4.5 billion now.
  • Education, transport and defence: each row costed on the same page, on the same basis.
  • Consultants and contracts: held flat, with no growth assumed.

These are the party's own numbers, produced by its own budget office. They are not an independent forecast, and we do not present them as one.

03

The papers

Request the working papers

Anyone can ask for the spreadsheets behind any figure on this site, and we will send them. An independent review of the costing is commissioned before the election, and its findings will be published here in full, in the order they arrive.
