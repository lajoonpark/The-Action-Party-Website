01

The standard

Tested, not asserted. The figures are the output of the last run.

What we hold ourselves to

The site targets WCAG 2.2 at level AA. That is a floor, not a badge: it is checked with automated tooling on every change, and the numbers below are what those checks return today.

Automated results
No violations at any tested width
Tested at
1440, 1200, 834, 390 and 320 pixels
Contrast
All text at or above 4.5 to 1
Zoom
Holds to 200 percent with no sideways scroll

Result

0

violations returned, at every width, on the last full run.

The audit covers contrast, names, roles, order and target size. It cannot judge whether the writing is clear, so that part is read by a person.

02

Keyboard

Every control on the site can be reached and operated without a mouse.

With a keyboard, or without a pointer

  • A skip link is the first thing in the tab order, so the menu can always be stepped over.
  • Focus rings are two pixels and never removed.
  • The policy deck and the front bench are reachable by Tab, and the arrow keys move between cards only while focus is inside them.
  • The mobile menu closes on Escape and returns focus to the button that opened it.
  • The sign-up confirmation is announced once, and can be dismissed with Escape or a click.

The ring

2px

Red, drawn outside the element, never removed and never covered by the element's own background.

Keys used

Tab and Shift-Tab to move, arrows inside the dial and the policy files, Enter or Space to act, Escape to close whatever opened.

03

Motion

Nothing moves that a reader has not asked to move.

If you ask for less movement

With reduced motion set in the operating system, every animation is removed rather than shortened, the reading progress rule is hidden, and smooth scrolling is turned off. Nothing that is hidden for a reveal stays hidden: all content is visible without scripting, because the reveal states are gated behind a class that only scripting adds.

The policy deck never takes the page's vertical scroll. A trackpad gesture that is mostly vertical moves the page, even when the finger drifts sideways.

Switched off

Reveals, parallax on the print plates, the dial's fill animation, the spinning ray field, the reading rule, and smooth scrolling.

What remains is the finished state: ink on stock, in place, at rest.

04

Problems

A fix is only a fix once it is written down.

If something does not work

Tell us, and we will fix it and record the fix in the public record. The contact route is the same one used for the working papers.

Report an accessibility problem

This is a demonstration site. The measurements above describe this build as tested, and the party it describes is fictional.

If you report one

It is logged the day it arrives, fixed in the next build, and named in the record with the date. No fix goes in quietly.
