What we would do

Five areas. Each one names the deadline and the trade-off, so you can decide whether the price is worth paying.

  1. I Health Care within the week, not the year 7 clauses

    Nobody waits twelve months for an operation they were told they needed in March. We will fund the public system to a guaranteed treatment time of seven days for urgent care and ninety for planned care, and publish the waiting list every fortnight.

    • Reopen 1,200 beds closed since 2019.
    • Move private patients out of public theatres by 2028, and say how.
    • Pay student nurses while they train, in exchange for four years of public service.
  2. II Housing Build where the transport already is 8 clauses

    Land held vacant inside existing town boundaries will be released for housing within twelve months, with one condition: the first home on every site must be affordable, and it must be finished before the last market home is sold.

    • No greenfield release while serviced infill land sits idle.
    • Five-year build deadlines written into every title.
    • Rent assistance indexed to actual rents, not a national average.
  3. III Work A wage nobody has to fall through 6 clauses

    The minimum wage should be a floor, not a negotiation. We will set it at 60% of the median wage, index it annually, and stop exempting the industries that lobby hardest against it.

    • Portable leave entitlements for casual and contract workers.
    • Wage theft enforced as a criminal offence, not a civil dispute.
    • Forty thousand training places funded in the first hundred days.
  4. IV Transport Fix what exists before breaking ground 5 clauses

    Before a new line is announced, every existing line gets a published reliability figure and a funded maintenance plan. New projects are approved only against a completed business case, published in full, in advance.

    • Two-year maintenance guarantee before any new corridor.
    • Bus networks re-tendered on frequency, not on cost alone.
    • Every project over $200 million goes to independent cost review.
  5. V Integrity Open books, closed revolving door 5 clauses

    Ministers' diaries, meetings and contracts will be published monthly. Former ministers will wait three years before lobbying the portfolio they ran, and donations will be disclosed in real time above a low threshold.

    • Live donation register, updated within seven days.
    • Three-year cooling-off period for ministers and senior staff.
    • A parliamentary budget office that answers to parliament, not the executive.

Four years

Measured against published forward estimates. Nothing is funded by growth we cannot name.
