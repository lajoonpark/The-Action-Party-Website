Collected

The honest total: what the forms do with your details.

What is collected

Nothing leaves your browser. The join, volunteer and donate forms validate what you type, tell you what would happen next, and then clear themselves. There is no server to receive the data, no database holding it, and no analytics script watching you read this page.

02

When live

What this page will say on the day the forms start working.

What changes when the forms are wired up

Before any real membership is taken, this page will say what is stored, where, for how long, and who can see it. It will name the retention period, the lawful basis, and the route to have your record corrected or removed. Those are not terms we can state today, because there is nothing yet to state them about, and writing them in advance would be exactly the kind of promise this party is built against.

What will not change: we do not sell member data, we do not swap it with other parties or campaigns, and leaving will take one click.

Unchanged

No sale of member data. No swaps with other parties or campaigns. One click to leave.

Those three are commitments, not settings, and they do not depend on what the form is built from.
